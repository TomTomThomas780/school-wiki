1. attitudes towards jobs 对工作的态度
2. hear of 听说
    hear of（听说某事/人，间接听说）；hear about（听说更多细节）；hear from（收到……来信）。
3. a journalist from China News
4. at the National library
5. book restoration 古籍修复
    restoration n.修复；动词 
    restore v.修复 
    book restorer n.古籍修复师
6. a damaged ancient book
7. deal with
8. due to 由于+名词，不能加句子
9. eat their way into books 蛀入书本
10. damage caused by… 由……造成的损坏
    caused by 是过去分词作后置定语，相当于定语从句 which was caused by；
    damage 不可数名词，“损害”。
11. What does it take to do sth.? 做某事需要具备什么条件？
12. in the past 在过去
    一般过去时标志词；in the past +时间段（in the past 10 years）则为现在完成时标志。
13. simple tools 简单工具
    hand tools 手工工具
    modern equipment
14. carry out 实施；开展
    carry out research/experiments/plans 开展研究/做实验/执行计划
15. do an operation on a patient 给病人做手术
    operation n.手术；
    operate v.做手术；搭配 
    operate on sb. 给某人做手术；
16. work as 担任；从事……工作
17. leave the army 退伍；离开军队
18. take an interest in 对……产生兴趣
19. for decades 数十年
    decade n.十年；decades = tens of years；“for + 时间段”，常搭配现在完成时
20. keep sb. going 支撑某人继续坚持下去
    keep + 宾语 + 宾补；keep sb. doing 让某人持续做某事；
21. feel bored 感到厌烦
22. take up this work 开始从事这项工作
    知识点：take up 短语含义：①开始从事（工作/爱好）；②占据（时间、空间）；
23. race against time 与时间赛跑
    against 介词，“与……对抗；反对”；race against sb./time 和某人赛跑/争分夺秒。
24. extra hours 额外时长；加班
extra adj.额外的；work extra hours 加班
25. educational backgrounds 教育背景
    background n.背景；educational adj.教育的；education n.教育
26. more scientific methods
27. help with digitalisation
28. would rather do sth. 宁愿做某事
    would rather + 动词原形；否定 would rather not do sth.；
    would rather do A than do B 宁愿做A不愿做B
29. pass down 传承；传递
pass sth. down (to sb.) 把某物传给某人。
