一、逐题讲解

Exercise 1 预约谈心 补全对话 · 5 题 · 每题 2 分

Situation Amy, a Grade 9 student, is making an appointment with Mr. Wang, her class teacher, to talk about her worries.
跳到题目12345
完整对话（答案已代入，上标数字为题号）

Amy: Good morning, Mr. Wang. Do you have a minute?

Mr Wang: Of course, Amy. You look worried today. 1What happened?

Amy: Since Grade 9 started, I have felt nervous. I often worry about tests, and sometimes I can't sleep well.

Mr Wang: I'm sorry to hear that. Many students feel the same at the beginning of Grade 9. I understand 2how you feel.

Amy: Thank you. I want to talk with you, but you are always busy. 3Can I talk with you this afternoon?

Mr Wang: Yes, this afternoon is OK. How about four o'clock after class?

Amy: That's great. 4Where shall we meet?

Mr Wang: Let's meet in my office.

Amy: OK. 5Do I need to prepare anything before I come?

Mr Wang: Yes. Just write down two or three things that worry you most. Then we can talk about them one by one.

Amy: Thank you, Mr Wang. I feel better now.

1. 提示：根据上下文询问情况
参考答案：What happened?What's the matter?What's wrong?
解题思路 下句 Amy 说 “Since Grade 9 started, I have felt nervous...”，是在讲述近况，故此处应询问“发生了什么事 / 怎么了”。What happened? / What's the matter? / What's wrong? 均可。
2. 提示：用宾语从句表示理解
参考答案：how you feel
解题思路 understand 是及物动词，后须接宾语从句；此处表示“我理解你的感受”，用 how you feel（连接副词 how 引导），与上句 Amy 描述的感受呼应。
3. 提示：预约谈话时间
参考答案：Can I talk with you this afternoon?Could I talk with you this afternoon?Are you free this afternoon?
解题思路 下文 Mr Wang 回答 “Yes, this afternoon is OK.”，可知此处是询问今天下午能否谈话。用 Can / Could I ...? 提出请求，或 Are you free ...? 询问是否有空。
4. 提示：询问见面地点
参考答案：Where shall we meet?Where can we meet?Where should we meet?
解题思路 下文回答 “Let's meet in my office.”，显然是询问见面地点。用 Where + shall / can / should we meet?
5. 提示：根据答句询问是否需要提前准备
参考答案：Do I need to prepare anything before I come?Should I write something down first?
解题思路 下文回答 “Just write down two or three things that worry you most.”，故此处询问是否需要提前准备。用 Do I need to ...? 或 Should I ...?

Exercise 2 职业日活动后交流 补全对话 · 5 题 · 每题 2 分

Situation Amy and Ben are talking after a Career Day activity at school.
跳到题目12345
完整对话（答案已代入，上标数字为题号）

Amy: Ben, which talk did you like best at Career Day?

Ben: I liked the engineer's talk best. The speaker showed us how a small idea could become a useful product.

Amy: Really? 1Why did you like it best?

Ben: Because the engineer can solve real problems and make people's lives easier.

Amy: That sounds meaningful. I'm interested in being a doctor, but I don't know 2whether I am suitable for the job.

Ben: Maybe you can join the hospital visit next month. It may help you learn more about the job.

Amy: Hospital visit? 3Have you ever taken part in a visit like that?

Ben: Yes, I have. I visited a children's hospital last term. It was really meaningful.

Amy: 4What did you do there?

Ben: We helped guide visitors and talked with some young patients. Before the visit, our teacher asked us to prepare some questions.

Amy: I see. By the way, I want to learn about other jobs, too.

Ben: There are some posters in the career corner. We can read them after school.

Amy: Great. 5Could we go to the career corner together?

Ben: Of course. Let's go there together after school.

1. 提示：询问原因
参考答案：Why did you like it best?
解题思路 下文 Ben 以 “Because ...” 开头作答，说明此处询问原因；结合上句 “which talk did you like best”，用 Why did you like it best?
2. 提示：用宾语从句表达不确定
参考答案：whether I am suitable for the jobwhether I can become a good doctor
解题思路 don't know 后接宾语从句表“不确定”，用 whether 引导；结合上句 “I'm interested in being a doctor”，表达“我是否适合这份工作 / 能否成为好医生”。
3. 提示：根据宾语从句问对方经历
参考答案：Have you ever taken part in a visit like that?Have you ever visited a hospital?
解题思路 下文回答 “Yes, I have. I visited a children's hospital last term.”，说明此处询问经历，用现在完成时 Have you ever ...?
4. 提示：根据下文提问
参考答案：What did you do there?
解题思路 下文回答 “We helped guide visitors and talked with some young patients.”，说明此处询问“（参观）那里时你们做了什么”，用 What did you do there?
5. 提示：发出邀请
参考答案：Could we go to the career corner together?Could you go to the career corner with me?Shall we go together?
解题思路 下文回答 “Of course. Let's go there together after school.”，故此处发出邀请，用 Could we ...? / Could you ... with me? / Shall we ...?

Exercise 3 检查学习计划 补全对话 · 5 题 · 每题 2 分

Situation Jack and Lily are checking Jack's study plan together.
跳到题目12345
完整对话（答案已代入，上标数字为题号）

Lily: Jack, you said your study plan didn't work well last week. 1Why didn't it work well?

Jack: Because I put too many tasks in one day. I felt tired and gave up.

Lily: You should change it.

Jack: I know. But I don't know 2what I should change first.

Lily: Start with the number of tasks. Choose only three important things each day.

Jack: That sounds possible. Which task should I put first?

Lily: Put the most difficult one first 3because you have more energy then.

Jack: Good point. I usually feel more energetic at the beginning.

Lily: Also, don't study for two hours without a break.

Jack: Then 4how often should I take a break?

Lily: Take a short break after every forty minutes. It can help you stay focused.

Jack: I'll try that. I also want to leave some time for exercise in the evening.

Lily: That's a good idea. Exercise can help you relax.

Jack: Then I'll put it into my plan 5so that I can relax after studying.

Lily: Great. This plan looks easier to follow now.

1. 提示：根据答句询问原因
参考答案：Why didn't it work well?Why didn't you follow it well?
解题思路 下句 Jack 以 “Because ...” 开头说明原因，故此处询问“为什么没效果”，用 Why didn't ...?
2. 提示：用宾语从句表达困惑
参考答案：what I should change firsthow I should change it
解题思路 don't know 后接宾语从句表困惑；下文 Lily 回答 “Start with the number of tasks.”，故询问“该先改什么 / 该怎么改”。
3. 提示：根据上下文说明这样安排的原因
参考答案：because you have more energy thenbecause you can do it better when you are not tired
解题思路 下句 Jack 说 “I usually feel more energetic at the beginning.”，故此处用 because 从句说明“此时精力更充沛”的原因。
4. 提示：根据答句询问休息安排
参考答案：how often should I take a break
解题思路 下文回答 “Take a short break after every forty minutes.”，故询问“多久休息一次”，用 how often 引导。
5. 提示：用目的状语从句补全句子
参考答案：so that I can relax after studyingso that I can stay healthy
解题思路 用 so that 引导目的状语从句；上文提到运动能帮助放松 / 保持健康，故补“以便学习后放松 / 保持健康”。

Exercise 4 读书会新书 补全对话 · 5 题 · 每题 2 分

Situation Linda and Mark are talking about a new book for the school reading club.
跳到题目12345
完整对话（答案已代入，上标数字为题号）

Linda: Mark, I saw you reading a new book yesterday. 1Have you finished it?

Mark: Yes, I have. I finished it last night. I really liked it.

Linda: 2What is the book about?

Mark: It's about a boy who keeps following his dream, even when others don't understand him.

Linda: That sounds interesting. Who wrote it?

Mark: A young writer from Shanghai. Her stories are close to teenagers' life.

Linda: No wonder many students like her books. I like stories 3that show teenagers' real feelings.

Mark: Then you may enjoy this one. The boy in the story changes a lot. At first, he is afraid to speak up, but later he becomes brave.

Linda: I wonder 4why he changes so much.

Mark: He meets a teacher who believes in him and encourages him.

Linda: That's moving. Are you going to introduce this book in the reading club?

Mark: Yes. I'll share it tomorrow.

Linda: 5Could I borrow it after you introduce it?

Mark: Sure. I'll bring it to the reading club tomorrow.

Linda: Thanks. I'm looking forward to reading it.

1. 提示：根据答句询问是否读完
参考答案：Have you finished it?
解题思路 下文回答 “Yes, I have. I finished it last night.”，故用现在完成时询问“你读完了吗”，Have you finished it?
2. 提示：根据答句提问
参考答案：What is the book about?
解题思路 下文回答 “It's about a boy who ...”，故询问“这本书是关于什么的”。
3. 提示：根据上文补全定语从句
参考答案：that show teenagers' real feelings
解题思路 stories 是先行词，后接定语从句，用 that / which 引导；上文说这位作家的故事 close to teenagers' life，故补“展现青少年真实情感的故事”。
4. 提示：用宾语从句根据下文提问
参考答案：why he changes so muchwhy he becomes brave later
解题思路 wonder 后接宾语从句；下文 Mark 回答 “He meets a teacher who believes in him and encourages him.”，故问“他为什么变化这么大 / 为什么后来变勇敢”。
5. 提示：根据答句编出借书请求
参考答案：Could I borrow it after you introduce it?Could I read it after you share it?
解题思路 下文回答 “Sure. I'll bring it to the reading club tomorrow.”，故此处提出借书请求，用 Could I ...?

Exercise 5 讨论如何正确使用 AI 学习助手 补全对话 · 5 题 · 每题 2 分

Situation Tony and Grace are discussing how to use an AI learning assistant properly.
跳到题目12345
完整对话（答案已代入，上标数字为题号）

Tony: Grace, have you tried the AI learning assistant on the school website?

Grace: Yes. It can help us review words and practise pronunciation.

Tony: Sounds useful. 1What else can it do?

Grace: It can also give suggestions after we finish a quiz.

Tony: 2What a helpful assistant!! But some students may ask it to do their homework.

Grace: That's not right. We shouldn't use it to finish our homework.

Tony: 3I agree. AI should help us learn, not replace our thinking.

Grace: Exactly. If we only copy answers from AI, 4we won't really learn.

Tony: Then what can we do first when we get stuck?

Grace: We can ask it for hints first. After that, we should try to work out the answer by ourselves.

Tony: I see. I wonder 5whether there are any rules about using AI.

Grace: Our teacher will show us some rules tomorrow.

Tony: Good. I'll take notes tomorrow. It'll help me remember the rules better.

1. 提示：根据答句询问其他功能
参考答案：What else can it do?
解题思路 下文回答 “It can also give suggestions after we finish a quiz.”，说明此处询问“它还能做什么（其他功能）”，用 What else can it do?
2. 提示：表示感叹
参考答案：What a helpful assistant!How helpful it is!
解题思路 表感叹，句式 What a / an + adj. + n.! 或 How + adj. + 主语 + 系动词!；针对上文 AI 助手的用处，感叹“多有用的助手啊”。
3. 提示：表示赞同
参考答案：I agree.I think so.You're right.
解题思路 上文 Grace 说 “We shouldn't use it to finish our homework.”，此处表示赞同，用 I agree. / I think so. / You're right.
4. 提示：补全条件句的结果
参考答案：we won't really learn.we will lose the chance to think.
解题思路 If 引导条件句，此处补主句结果；结合上句 “AI should help us learn, not replace our thinking.”，表达“若只抄答案，我们就学不到 / 会失去思考的机会”。
5. 提示：根据答句提问
参考答案：whether there are any rules about using AI.whether we have any rules about using AI.
解题思路 下文回答 “Our teacher will show us some rules tomorrow.”，故问“是否有关于使用 AI 的规则”，用 whether 引导的宾语从句。

Exercise 6 社区阅览室志愿活动 补全对话 · 5 题 · 每题 2 分

Situation Jenny and Peter are talking about a volunteer activity in a community reading room.
跳到题目12345
完整对话（答案已代入，上标数字为题号）

Jenny: Peter, our class is going to visit a community reading room this Saturday.

Peter: 1What will we do there?

Jenny: We will read stories to young children and help put books in order.

Peter: That sounds meaningful. I have never read stories to children before. 2Do I need to prepare anything?

Jenny: You'd better choose a short story and practise reading it aloud.

Peter: Good idea. I have a picture book at home. Maybe children will like it.

Jenny: 3I think so. The activity will be held in the community reading room.

Peter: Got it. 4When and where shall we meet?

Jenny: We'll meet at the school gate at 8:30. The activity starts at 9:10.

Peter: OK. I think David may also be interested in this activity.

Jenny: Really? He likes reading stories, doesn't he?

Peter: Yes. 5Can I invite him to join us?

Jenny: Of course. More volunteers will make the activity better.

Peter: Great. I'll ask him after class.

1. 提示：根据答句提问
参考答案：What will we do there?What are we going to do there?
解题思路 下文回答 “We will read stories to young children and help put books in order.”，故询问“我们在那里做什么”。
2. 提示：根据答句提问
参考答案：Do I need to prepare anything?Should I prepare something before Saturday?
解题思路 下文回答 “You'd better choose a short story and practise reading it aloud.”，故询问“是否需要准备什么”。
3. 提示：表示赞同
参考答案：I think so.I agree with you.
解题思路 上文 Peter 说 “Maybe children will like it.”，此处表示赞同，用 I think so. / I agree with you.
4. 提示：询问集合安排
参考答案：When and where shall we meet?What time and where shall we meet?
解题思路 下文回答 “We'll meet at the school gate at 8:30.”，故询问“何时何地集合”。
5. 提示：根据答句询问能否邀请同学参加
参考答案：Can I invite him to join us?Could I ask him to join us?
解题思路 下文回答 “Of course. More volunteers will make the activity better.”，故询问能否邀请同学参加，用 Can / Could I ...?
