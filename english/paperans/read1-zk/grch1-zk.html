语篇（答案已代入，数字为题号）

Winter is here, and many people are planning their holidays. Tom, a 10-year-old boy, has a special plan for his winter break. He wants to spend his holidays 1with his family and friends. First, he plans to visit the mountains to enjoy the snow and try 2something new.

Tom's holiday plan starts with a trip to the mountains. He loves snow and wants to try skiing for the first time. His brother will take him to a ski resort, where he 3can learn how to ski from a teacher. Tom is very excited because he has 4never seen so much snow before. After his ski adventure, Tom's family will explore 5other fun places.

After skiing, Tom and his family will visit a nearby town. The town is 6famous for its beautiful Christmas lights. Every year, the town is decorated with colorful lights, and many people come to see them. Tom can't wait to walk through the town and enjoy the bright lights.

7In addition to visiting the town, Tom also plans to spend some quiet time at home, for example, reading books and watching his favorite movies. He loves winter stories, especially 8those about snowy days and magical adventures.

Finally, he plans to enjoy his time with friends and make the most of the winter season. During the holiday, Tom plans to visit his friends. He and his friends 9will play in the snow, build snowmen, and have snowball fights. They will also make hot chocolate and enjoy the winter weather together. Overall, Tom is looking forward to 10a fun and exciting winter holiday!

1. 本题考查：介词搭配（spend…with）
He wants to spend his holidays 1with his family and friends.
A. inB. onC. with
答案：C. with
本题考点 spend time with sb. 表示「与某人一起度过（时间）」，用介词 with 表伴随。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. in 表示「在……里面」，不能表示「和某人一起」；
  • B. on 表示「在……上／关于」，也不能表示伴随。
2. 本题考查：不定代词 + 形容词后置
First, he plans to visit the mountains to enjoy the snow and try 2something new.
A. somethingB. anythingC. everything
答案：A. something
本题考点 形容词修饰不定代词时要后置，something new「某种新东西」；肯定句中用 something。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. anything 多用于否定句或疑问句；
  • C. everything「一切」与「尝试新事物」的语境不符。
3. 本题考查：情态动词
His brother will take him to a ski resort, where he 3can learn how to ski from a teacher.
A. needB. canC. must
答案：B. can
本题考点 where 引导的定语从句中，he can learn「他能够学」用 can 表能力。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. need「需要」语义不符，此处并非「需要学」；
  • C. must「必须」语气过强，与轻松的记叙语气不符。
4. 本题考查：副词 + 现在完成时
Tom is very excited because he has 4never seen so much snow before.
A. justB. everC. never
答案：C. never
本题考点 never … before「以前从未……」，与 has 构成现在完成时，说明从没见这么多雪。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. just 表「刚刚」，与 before 及 excited 的原因不搭；
  • B. ever 多用于疑问句或表「曾经」，此处为否定含义。
5. 本题考查：other / another / others 辨析
After his ski adventure, Tom's family will explore 5other fun places.
A. otherB. anotherC. others
答案：A. other
本题考点 other + 复数名词 =「其他的……」，other fun places「其他好玩的地方」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. another 后接单数名词，不能接复数的 places；
  • C. others 是代词，后面不能再接名词。
6. 本题考查：be famous for
The town is 6famous for its beautiful Christmas lights.
A. well-knowB. famousC. interesting
答案：B. famous
本题考点 be famous for「以……闻名」，固定搭配。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. well-know 形式有误（形容词应为 well-known），排除；
  • C. be interesting 不能与 for 构成「以……闻名」。
7. 本题考查：介词短语 + 动名词
7In addition to visiting the town, Tom also plans to spend some quiet time at home, for example, reading books and watching his favorite movies.
A. In addition toB. Instead ofC. Look forward to
答案：A. In addition to
本题考点 In addition to「除……之外（还）」，后接动名词，符合「除了游览小镇，还……」的递进关系。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. Instead of「代替／而不是」表相反关系，与句意（两件事都做）不符；
  • C. Look forward to 是谓语动词短语，不能作句首介词短语。
8. 本题考查：指示代词
He loves winter stories, especially 8those about snowy days and magical adventures.
A. thatB. thoseC. these
答案：B. those
本题考点 指代前文提到的复数名词 winter stories，用 those（= those stories）。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. that 指代单数或不可数名词；
  • C. these 表近指「这些」，指上文已提到的那些故事用 those 更合适。
9. 本题考查：一般将来时
He and his friends 9will play in the snow, build snowmen, and have snowball fights.
A. playB. are playingC. will play
答案：C. will play
本题考点 本段讲假期计划，且与后面的 will also make 一致，用一般将来时 will play。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. play 一般现在时与「计划要做」的语境不符；
  • B. are playing 现在进行时不能表示假期将要做的事。
10. 本题考查：冠词
Overall, Tom is looking forward to 10a fun and exciting winter holiday!
A. /B. theC. a
答案：C. a
本题考点 首次提到的可数名词单数前用不定冠词 a；holiday 是可数名词单数。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. 零冠词（不填）不能用于可数名词单数前；
  • B. the 表特指，此处 holiday 首次提到，不特指。

语篇（答案已代入，数字为题号）

In recent years, many people have started 1enjoying food shows on their phones or computers, especially during mealtimes. These shows are not only fun to watch but also help people learn about cultures 2through food.

Chen, a 20-year-old college student in Guangzhou, always watches a food program while eating lunch. Her favorite show is about two young travelers 3traveling around Southeast Asia to taste street food. “I love seeing 4how different people cook and eat. It makes my lunch more enjoyable,” she said.

Zhang Ming, a middle school teacher in Nanjing, enjoys 5watching food videos before going to bed. “It relaxes me. The sounds of cooking and the beautiful food make me feel calm,” she said. Her favorite is a baking show 6that teaches viewers how to make cakes and cookies.

Lisa from the UK has been living in China 7for five years. She enjoys Chinese food shows and finds them helpful. “I 8used to be afraid of trying new dishes, but now I understand them better. Food programs help me feel closer to Chinese culture,” she said.

Omar, 9an exchange student from Egypt, watches food shows when he feels homesick. “Sometimes I even try to cook the same dishes I see in the shows,” he said with a smile.

Colorful food, relaxing music, and interesting stories make food shows popular all over the world. No matter 10where we come from, we can all feel connected through the love of food. It's more than just eating — it's a way to share happiness.

1. 本题考查：start doing
In recent years, many people have started 1enjoying food shows on their phones or computers, especially during mealtimes.
A. enjoyB. to enjoyingC. enjoying
答案：C. enjoying
本题考点 start doing sth.（= start to do sth.）「开始做某事」。注意选项 B「to enjoying」本身搭配错误（to 后不能接动名词）。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. enjoy 原形不能直接跟在 have started 后；
  • B. to enjoying 是错误搭配，正确应为 to enjoy 或 enjoying。
2. 本题考查：介词（through）
These shows are not only fun to watch but also help people learn about cultures 2through food.
A. throughB. forC. with
答案：A. through
本题考点 through「通过（某种方式）」，through food「通过食物」表媒介。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. for「为了」表目的，语义不符；
  • C. with 多指「用（具体工具）」，此处「通过食物了解文化」用 through 更贴切。
3. 本题考查：现在分词作后置定语
Her favorite show is about two young travelers 3traveling around Southeast Asia to taste street food.
A. travel aroundB. traveling aroundC. will travel around
答案：B. traveling around
本题考点 现在分词短语作后置定语，说明 travelers 正在进行的动作（= who are traveling around…）。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. travel around 原形不能作定语；
  • C. will travel around 是谓语形式，不能作定语。
4. 本题考查：宾语从句连接词
“I love seeing 4how different people cook and eat.
A. whyB. whenC. how
答案：C. how
本题考点 how 引导宾语从句，表示「怎样」——看不同的人怎样做饭、吃饭。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. why「为什么」与「看别人做饭吃饭」不符；
  • B. when「何时」也不符，语境强调的是方式。
5. 本题考查：enjoy doing
Zhang Ming, a middle school teacher in Nanjing, enjoys 5watching food videos before going to bed.
A. watchB. watchingC. to watch
答案：B. watching
本题考点 enjoy doing sth.「喜欢做某事」，固定接动名词。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. watch 原形不能作 enjoy 的宾语；
  • C. enjoy 后不接不定式 to watch。
6. 本题考查：定语从句关系代词
Her favorite is a baking show 6that teaches viewers how to make cakes and cookies.
A. thatB. whenC. who
答案：A. that
本题考点 先行词 a baking show 是物，且在从句中作主语，用关系代词 that（= which）。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. when 是关系副词，作时间状语，此处从句缺主语；
  • C. who 指人，先行词是物，不能用。
7. 本题考查：for + 时间段
Lisa from the UK has been living in China 7for five years.
A. sinceB. forC. during
答案：B. for
本题考点 for + 一段时间，用于现在完成进行时，表示持续了五年。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. since 后接时间点（如 since 2019），不能接 five years；
  • C. during + 名词，表「在……期间」，不表持续时长。
8. 本题考查：used to do
“I 8used to be afraid of trying new dishes, but now I understand them better.
A. am usedB. am usingC. used
答案：C. used
本题考点 used to do sth.「过去常常做某事」，与后半句 now 形成今昔对比。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. be used to 是「习惯于」，后应接名词／动名词，不能接 to be afraid；
  • B. am using 是「正在使用」，语义不通。
9. 本题考查：不定冠词 a/an
Omar, 9an exchange student from Egypt, watches food shows when he feels homesick.
A. anB. aC. the
答案：A. an
本题考点 exchange 以元音音素开头，用 an；此处作同位语，泛指「一名」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. a 用于辅音音素开头的词前；
  • C. the 表特指，此处首次提到，用不定冠词。
10. 本题考查：no matter + 疑问词
No matter 10where we come from, we can all feel connected through the love of food.
A. whenB. whereC. how
答案：B. where
本题考点 no matter where 引导让步状语从句，「无论我们从哪里来」，与 come from 呼应。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. no matter when「无论何时」与 come from 不搭；
  • C. no matter how「无论怎样」不能与 come from 搭配。

语篇（答案已代入，数字为题号）

Have you ever 1heard of upcycling? It is a way of turning old or unnecessary things into something useful and often beautiful. Is it the same 2as recycling, which usually takes old things and breaks them down to make a new product? When you upcycle an object, you 3are making a greener choice. You can simply remake it. For example, you 4might make a bag out of an old T-shirt. Also, the upcycled object is usually in the same, or even better condition.

Upcycling isn't a ready-made idea. Many ideas behind modern-day upcycling 5come from the past. In those days, things were repurposed over and over 6until they were no longer useful. Upcycling is a way of life. Things shouldn't be just 7thrown away when they can be reused and turned into something useful.

It's clear that upcycling is green. It is also considered to be more 8environmentally friendly than recycling. Upcycling can just require your own ideas and work, but recycling requires energy or water 9to process materials.

In fact, no matter whether you create objects from old things or buy ready-made products from upcycled things, 10both ways are helpful for the environment and can bring you something that's beautiful and useful. Get started today!

1. 本题考查：现在完成时（过去分词）
Have you ever 1heard of upcycling?
A. hearB. heardC. hears
答案：B. heard
本题考点 现在完成时 have + 过去分词，hear 的过去分词是 heard，have heard of「听说过」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. hear 原形不能用于 have 之后；
  • C. hears 是第三人称单数，不用于完成时。
2. 本题考查：the same as
Is it the same 2as recycling, which usually takes old things and breaks them down to make a new product?
A. forB. likeC. as
答案：C. as
本题考点 the same as「和……一样」，固定搭配。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. the same for 无此搭配；
  • B. the same like 不成立，like 表「像」但不能构成此固定搭配。
3. 本题考查：现在进行时
When you upcycle an object, you 3are making a greener choice.
A. are makingB. makeC. have made
答案：A. are making
本题考点 when 引导的时间状语从句中，主句用现在进行时，表示「当你……时，你其实正在做一个更环保的选择」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. make 一般现在时不如进行时贴切，无法体现动作正在发生；
  • C. have made 现在完成时强调结果，与「当你做时」的即时性不符。
4. 本题考查：情态动词（表可能）
For example, you 4might make a bag out of an old T-shirt.
A. mustB. shouldC. might
答案：C. might
本题考点 might 表「可以／有可能」，用于举例说明一种可行做法：「你可以把旧 T 恤做成一个包」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. must「必须」语气过强；
  • B. should「应该」表建议，语境是举例说明「可以」，用 might 更自然。
5. 本题考查：主谓一致
Many ideas behind modern-day upcycling 5come from the past.
A. coming fromB. come fromC. comes from
答案：B. come from
本题考点 主语 Many ideas 是复数，谓语用动词原形 come from。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. coming from 是非谓语形式，句子缺谓语；
  • C. comes from 是第三人称单数，与复数主语不一致。
6. 本题考查：连词 until
In those days, things were repurposed over and over 6until they were no longer useful.
A. unlessB. untilC. if
答案：B. until
本题考点 until「直到……为止」：反复利用，直到它们不再有用。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. unless「除非」表条件，语义不符；
  • C. if「如果」表条件，也不符。
7. 本题考查：被动语态
Things shouldn't be just 7thrown away when they can be reused and turned into something useful.
A. thrown awayB. threw awayC. throw away
答案：A. thrown away
本题考点 被动语态 be + 过去分词；throw 的过去分词是 thrown，thrown away「被扔掉」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. threw away 是过去式，不能用于被动；
  • C. throw away 是原形，不能用于被动。
8. 本题考查：副词修饰形容词
It is also considered to be more 8environmentally friendly than recycling.
A. environmentB. environmentalC. environmentally
答案：C. environmentally
本题考点 副词 environmentally 修饰形容词 friendly，构成 environmentally friendly「环保的」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. environment 是名词；
  • B. environmental 是形容词，不能修饰 friendly。
9. 本题考查：不定式表目的
Upcycling can just require your own ideas and work, but recycling requires energy or water 9to process materials.
A. to processB. processingC. process
答案：A. to process
本题考点 require sth. to do sth.，不定式表目的：「需要能源或水来处理材料」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. processing 动名词不能直接跟在 water 后表目的；
  • C. process 原形不能直接用于此处。
10. 本题考查：不定代词 both/neither/either
In fact, no matter whether you create objects from old things or buy ready-made products from upcycled things, 10both ways are helpful for the environment and can bring you something that's beautiful and useful.
A. neitherB. bothC. either
答案：B. both
本题考点 both ways「两种方式都……」，both 指「两者都」，谓语用复数 are。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. neither「两者都不」与 helpful 及 are 矛盾；
  • C. either「两者之一」，谓语应用单数。

1. 本题考查：并列连词（转折）
Building friendships is a lifelong 1but worthwhile task.
A. soB. orC. but
答案：C. but

其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. so 表因果；
  • B. or 表选择，均与句意不符。
2. 本题考查：动词（join）
● 2Join classes, clubs, or volunteer organizations.
A. Sign upB. JoinC. Enter for
答案：B. Join
本题考点 join + 组织／团体「加入」；join classes/clubs「参加（加入）课程、社团」。

  • A. sign up 需与 for 连用（sign up for classes）；
  • C. enter for 是「报名参加（比赛）」，搭配不当。
3. 本题考查：反身代词
● If you want to make friends, you first need to put 3yourself out there somehow in order to meet people.
A. yourselfB. myselfC. ourselves
答案：A. yourself
本题考点 主语是 you，反身代词用 yourself；put yourself out there「让自己主动走出去（接触别人）」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. myself 与主语 you 不一致；
  • C. ourselves 是复数反身代词，与 you 不一致。
4. 本题考查：情态动词（表可能）
If you just sit alone, friends 4might come to you — but that's not likely.
A. mightB. mustC. should
答案：A. might
本题考点 might 表可能性（较小），与后半句 but that's not likely 呼应。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. must「必须／一定」语气过强；
  • C. should「应该」表义务或推测，不如 might 贴切。
5. 本题考查：频度副词
Remember, friends 5seldom come knocking on your door while you sit at home playing on your laptop.
A. oftenB. seldomC. quite
答案：B. seldom
本题考点 seldom「很少」，与语境（坐在家里玩电脑，朋友很少会上门）一致。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. often「经常」与语境矛盾；
  • C. quite 是程度副词，不能修饰 come。
6. 本题考查：look 短语辨析
If you have an argument, try to 6look at things from the other person's point of view.
A. look atB. look forC. look into
答案：A. look at
本题考点 look at「看待、审视」，look at things from sb's point of view「站在他人角度看问题」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. look for「寻找」；
  • C. look into「调查」，均与「从……角度看」不搭。
7. 本题考查：宾语从句连接词
Take the time to listen to 7what they're saying, and offer advice only if they ask for it.
A. whatB. howC. why
答案：A. what
本题考点 what 引导宾语从句，作 saying 的宾语：「听他们在说什么」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. how「怎样」、
  • C. why「为什么」都不能作 saying 的宾语。
8. 本题考查：否定助动词
● Respect the other person's opinion, even when you 8don't agree.
A. won'tB. don'tC. haven't
答案：B. don't
本题考点 when 引导的让步状语从句用一般现在时：even when you don't agree「即使你不同意」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. won't 表意愿或将来，不合语境；
  • C. haven't agree 搭配错误（have 后应接过去分词）。
9. 本题考查：other / another / others
If your friend tells you something personal or private, don't share it with 9other people.
A. othersB. anotherC. other
答案：C. other
本题考点 other + 复数名词 =「其他的……」，other people「其他人」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. others 是代词，后面不能再接 people；
  • B. another 后接单数名词。
10. 本题考查：定语从句关系代词
If your friend is involved in an activity 10that they care about, show your support and interest.
A. whatB. whoC. that
答案：C. that
本题考点 先行词 an activity 是物，在从句中作 care about 的宾语，用关系代词 that（= which）。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. what 不能引导定语从句；
  • B. who 指人，先行词是物。

Passage 5 说明文 · 篇幅 234 · 难度★★ · 人与社会（科学与技术）

跳到题目12345678910
语篇（答案已代入，数字为题号）

AI is shaking up the world now. It has been widely used in many different fields.

AI in Daily Life

Chatbots, a kind of AI-driven tool, 1have become an important part of modern communication. They can understand and reply to users at once. Whether it's on websites 2or mobile apps, chatbots can offer support in real time. 3Besides, users can control smart home tools through voice commands. AI is reshaping the way we communicate in our daily lives.

AI in Transportation

AI makes a big difference to traffic. For example, some ride-sharing platforms depend 4heavily on AI. They use AI 5to match passengers with drivers and provide better transportation services. And self-driving cars are a main example of AI's use in transportation, too. With the help of AI, they 6can help reduce accidents and help those unable to drive.

AI in Education

AI provides a smart way to learn. It changes 7the way teachers teach and students learn. AI in classrooms can make teaching and learning 8more interesting, which helps students focus on their studies. AI can help with homework. For example, 9it can help with homework, answer questions, and provide explanations when students study at home. 10By analyzing students' advantages, weaknesses and learning needs, AI can make suitable study plans for them. This can help students achieve their learning goals more easily.

All in all, AI is shaping and will continue to shape our everyday experiences.

1. 本题考查：主谓一致 + 时态
Chatbots, a kind of AI-driven tool, 1have become an important part of modern communication.
A. has becomeB. have becomeC. became
答案：B. have become
本题考点 主语是 Chatbots（复数），谓语用 have become；a kind of AI-driven tool 只是同位语，不影响主谓一致。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. has become 误按同位语的单数来选，主谓不一致；
  • C. became 一般过去时与现在语境不符。
2. 本题考查：whether…or…
Whether it's on websites 2or mobile apps, chatbots can offer support in real time.
A. orB. forC. and
答案：A. or
本题考点 whether…or…「无论是……还是……」，固定结构。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. for 不能与 whether 搭配；
  • C. and 不构成 whether…or 结构。
3. 本题考查：连接副词
3Besides, users can control smart home tools through voice commands.
A. InsteadB. ThoughC. Besides
答案：C. Besides
本题考点 Besides「此外（而且）」承上补充：除能即时提供帮助外，还能控制智能家居。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. Instead「相反」表转折；
  • B. Though「尽管」表让步，均不符。
4. 本题考查：副词修饰动词
For example, some ride-sharing platforms depend 4heavily on AI.
A. heavilyB. nearlyC. simply
答案：A. heavily
本题考点 heavily 修饰 depend on，depend heavily on「严重依赖」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. nearly「几乎」与 depend on 不搭；
  • C. simply「仅仅」语义不符（语境强调依赖程度深）。
5. 本题考查：不定式表目的
They use AI 5to match passengers with drivers and provide better transportation services.
A. matchB. to matchC. matching
答案：B. to match
本题考点 use sth. to do sth.，不定式表目的：用 AI 来把乘客和司机匹配起来。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. match 原形不能表目的；
  • C. matching 动名词不能直接跟在 AI 后表目的。
6. 本题考查：情态动词
With the help of AI, they 6can help reduce accidents and help those unable to drive.
A. mustB. needC. can
答案：C. can
本题考点 can 表能力／可能：「它们能够帮助减少事故」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. must「必须」语气过强；
  • B. need「需要」语义不符。
7. 本题考查：冠词 the way
It changes 7the way teachers teach and students learn.
A. /B. aC. the
答案：C. the
本题考点 the way…「……的方式」，特指「老师教、学生学的方式」，用定冠词 the。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. 零冠词不用于此处特指；
  • B. a 表泛指，不合语境。
8. 本题考查：make + 宾补（形容词）
AI in classrooms can make teaching and learning 8more interesting, which helps students focus on their studies.
A. interestedB. more interestingC. more interested
答案：B. more interesting
本题考点 make + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补；teaching and learning 是事物，用 interesting；比较级 more interesting 表示比以前更有趣。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. interested 表「（人）感兴趣的」，不能形容事物；
  • C. more interested 指「人更感兴趣」，与主语（教学）不符。
9. 本题考查：代词 it
For example, 9it can help with homework, answer questions, and provide explanations when students study at home.
A. itB. thisC. they
答案：A. it
本题考点 it 指代前文的 AI，作主语：AI can help with homework… For example, it can help…
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. this 一般指代上文整句内容，此处需要指代 AI 单数的代词；
  • C. they 是复数，与 AI 单数不一致。
10. 本题考查：by + 动名词（方式）
10By analyzing students' advantages, weaknesses and learning needs, AI can make suitable study plans for them.
A. WithB. ForC. By
答案：C. By
本题考点 by + 动名词，表方式／手段：「通过分析……，AI 能……」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. with 一般接名词／名词短语，with analyzing 不自然；
  • B. for 表目的，语义不符。

Passage 6 议论文 · 篇幅 207 · 难度★★★ · 人与社会（历史、社会与文化）

语篇（答案已代入，数字为题号）

Online food delivery is growing so rapidly that almost everyone in China knows about it, and many young people even say it is hard to live without it. In fact, online food delivery services are having a huge influence 1on our society.

Many disadvantages of online food delivery services shouldn't 2be forgotten. Firstly, they make it 3even easier to order unhealthy food, high in sugar, fat and salt. As a matter of fact, fast food is among the most commonly 4ordered types of food in the world as it doesn't take much time to prepare and deliver. Food safety is 5another problem: it's uncertain 6whether the ingredients are fresh. There are also concerns about putting hot food in plastic boxes, which are 7widely used in the food delivery industry. This has a terrible effect on the environment. According to research, more than 8a million tons of packaging waste from online food delivery are generated in China every year.

Online food delivery really has a great 9effect on our daily life. If you are not busy, it's better 10to go shopping, buy some fresh ingredients and cook them on your own, which can not only reduce waste, especially packaging waste, but also allow you to enjoy life.

1. 本题考查：have an influence on
In fact, online food delivery services are having a huge influence 1on our society.
A. inB. forC. on
答案：C. on
本题考点 have an influence on「对……有影响」，固定搭配。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. have an influence in 不成立；
  • B. have an influence for 搭配不当。
2. 本题考查：被动语态
Many disadvantages of online food delivery services shouldn't 2be forgotten.
A. forgetB. forgotC. be forgotten
答案：C. be forgotten
本题考点 主语 disadvantages 是被忘记的对象，用被动语态 shouldn't be forgotten；forget 的过去分词是 forgotten。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. forget 主动形式与主语（被忘记的缺点）不符；
  • B. forgot 是过去式，不能用在情态动词后。
3. 本题考查：even + 比较级
Firstly, they make it 3even easier to order unhealthy food, high in sugar, fat and salt.
A. evenB. veryC. quite
答案：A. even
本题考点 even + 比较级「甚至更……」，even easier 修饰比较级，加强程度。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. very 不能直接修饰比较级；
  • C. quite 也不能修饰比较级。
4. 本题考查：过去分词作定语
As a matter of fact, fast food is among the most commonly 4ordered types of food in the world as it doesn't take much time to prepare and deliver.
A. orderB. orderedC. ordering
答案：B. ordered
本题考点 过去分词作定语表被动：「最常被点的食物种类」，food is ordered。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. order 原形不能作定语；
  • C. ordering 现在分词表主动，食物是被点，应用过去分词。
5. 本题考查：another
Food safety is 5another problem: it's uncertain 6whether the ingredients are fresh.
A. othersB. anotherC. the other
答案：B. another
本题考点 another + 单数名词「另一个（问题）」，指众多问题中的又一个。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. others 是代词，后不能接 problem；
  • C. the other 指两者中的另一个，此处不限于两者。
6. 本题考查：whether 引导主语从句
Food safety is 5another problem: it's uncertain 6whether the ingredients are fresh.
A. ifB. whetherC. that
答案：B. whether
本题考点 whether 引导主语从句（it 是形式主语）：「原料是否新鲜还不确定」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. if 一般不用于此处的主语从句引导，规范用法用 whether；
  • C. that 引导陈述性从句，但句意需要一个「是否」。
7. 本题考查：副词修饰动词
There are also concerns about putting hot food in plastic boxes, which are 7widely used in the food delivery industry.
A. wideB. widenC. widely
答案：C. widely
本题考点 副词 widely 修饰 used，widely used「被广泛使用」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. wide 是形容词，不能修饰 used；
  • B. widen 是动词。
8. 本题考查：数字表达
According to research, more than 8a million tons of packaging waste from online food delivery are generated in China every year.
A. a millionB. millionC. millions
答案：A. a million
本题考点 具体数量用 a million（= one million）+ 复数名词：「超过一百万吨」。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. million 前无冠词或数词，用法不完整；
  • C. millions 应与 of 连用（millions of），不能直接接 tons。
9. 本题考查：have an effect on
Online food delivery really has a great 9effect on our daily life.
A. effectB. resultC. affect
答案：A. effect
本题考点 have an effect on「对……有影响」，effect 是名词。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • B. result 的搭配是 result in/of，无 have a result on；
  • C. affect 是动词，此处需要名词。
10. 本题考查：it is better to do
If you are not busy, it's better 10to go shopping, buy some fresh ingredients and cook them on your own, which can not only reduce waste, especially packaging waste, but also allow you to enjoy life.
A. goB. to goC. going
答案：B. to go
本题考点 it is better to do sth.「做某事更好」，it 是形式主语，真正主语是不定式。
其他选项不正确的原因
  • A. go 原形不能作主语；
  • C. 此处用不定式作真正主语，不用动名词。
